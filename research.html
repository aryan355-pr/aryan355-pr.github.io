Diagnosing Adaptation Failure Modes in Foundation Models for RNFL Thickness Prediction

Oral @ MICCAI OMIA 2026

Venue: 13th Ophthalmic Medical Image Analysis Workshop (MICCAI 2026) · Format: Oral Paper

Standard classification-centric adaptation protocols (progressive layer freezing, Pearson losses) paradoxically degrade continuous dense metric regression by 18–32% on en face IR-SLO fundus imaging. We identify two distinct failure modes in foundation models like RETFound (ViT-L/16) and demonstrate that a first-order gradient penalty probe restores patient-specific prediction variance ($\sigma_{\text{pred}} \approx 11.8\,\mu\text{m}$) without compromising global accuracy ($19.04 \pm 0.03\,\mu\text{m}$).
